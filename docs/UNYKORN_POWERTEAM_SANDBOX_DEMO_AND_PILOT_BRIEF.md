# UNYKORN × POWERTEAM Interactive Sandbox Demonstration & Pilot Configuration Brief

## Executive Opening Statement (For Kevin Harrington & Bill Walsh)

> *"What you are viewing is a private, interactive sandbox demonstration created by Unykorn to show how a Powerteam-style member ecosystem could be modernized. It is not a live Powerteam product, event checkout system, credit program, custody solution, speaker agreement, or investment offering.*
> 
> *The workflows shown are built from reusable templates and working prototype components: member access, digital credentials, event check-in, prepaid service-credit ledgering, concierge automation, reconciliation reporting, and controlled settlement logic. With Powerteam’s authorization, operational inputs, commercial terms, and legal review, we can configure and deploy the approved components for a real pilot."*

---

## 1. What This Is
A private interactive demonstration showing how Powerteam’s events, coaching, education, media, and member experience could operate through a unified digital platform.

## 2. What Is Already Built
A reusable Passport interface, member credential model, event check-in pattern, service-credit ledger architecture, reconciliation framework, administrative workflows, AI concierge tooling, and prototype smart-contract components.

## 3. What Is Simulated
Names, event inventory, prices, balances, speaker references, media access, payment flows, custody references, settlement examples, and program benefits are illustrative unless separately approved in writing.

## 4. What Can Be Configured
One approved event or program, branded member portal, member tiers, authorized catalog, event check-in, verified participation records, support workflows, CRM/LMS/payment integrations, and operational reporting.

## 5. What Requires Approval First
Brand/IP permission, product and event ownership, customer terms, pricing, payment processor setup, privacy review, security review, staff operations, speaker/media rights, and any financial or regulated-service design.

## 6. Recommended First Pilot
* **Scope:** One authorized program SKU (e.g. Dania Beach, Orlando, or a specific mastermind cohort).
* **Scale:** 50–100 invited participants.
* **Access Mode:** Invitation-only registration with authenticated digital credentials.
* **Boundaries:** No token transfers, no cash redemption, no investment product.
* **Success Criteria:** Verified member check-in speed, engagement tracking, zero attendee confusion, and clean post-event finance reconciliation.

## 8. Demo Walkthrough Script Guide

### Step 1: Member Experience
> *"This is the member-facing experience. It shows how an attendee could receive a branded Passport, see eligible programs, access a controlled check-in credential, and retain a verified participation record.*
> 
> *This rotating QR is a sandbox representation of the check-in control. In production, it would be connected to the approved event, authenticated staff scanner, event-access rules, and audit log."*

### Step 2: Service Credits and Reconciliation
> *"This section demonstrates a controlled prepaid-service-credit and reconciliation model. It can help centralize entitlement tracking, reduce manual spreadsheet work, and give operations clearer visibility into credits, refunds, attendance, and service usage.*
> 
> *The displayed balance, tiers, values, and catalog items are illustrative. A production credit program would use Powerteam-approved terms, pricing, refund rules, inventory, tax handling, payment processor integration, and daily finance reconciliation."*

### Step 3: Content, Speakers, and Rights
> *"This area demonstrates how authorized speaker, media, curriculum, and program entitlements could be organized in one member experience. Future partner reporting or payment workflows would be configured only from signed agreements and finance-approved rules.*
> 
> *Names, images, event references, and media displayed here are for architectural simulation only. Their inclusion does not represent endorsement, participation, commercial terms, or a live integration."*

### Step 4: Pilot Proposal & Next Step
> *"Choose one authorized use case—such as a single event, coaching cohort, or approved media library—and define the pilot’s business rules, integrations, success metrics, support model, and approvals.*
> 
> *If the operating model fits, we can take one approved program SKU and configure a controlled pilot from this template. The goal is to prove member activation, check-in, engagement, and reconciliation—not to launch a financial product."*
