# 🚀 Pilot Runbook — CEO Success Summit Pilot

## 1. Pilot Scope & Constraints
* **Target Event**: Single identified event (e.g., CEO Success Summit — Fort Lauderdale / Las Vegas).
* **Cohort Cap**: 50–100 beta participants.
* **Onboarding Path**: Email sign-in + embedded account abstraction wallet.
* **Payment Settlement**: 100% card/Stripe settled prior to credit allocation.

## 2. Success Metrics & Key Performance Indicators
1. **Activation Rate**: $\ge 80\%$ of issued Passports successfully activated and authenticated.
2. **Reconciliation Accuracy**: $< 1\%$ variance across payments, credits issued, and redeemed SKU balances.
3. **Zero Double-Mints**: $0$ duplicate issuances or redemptions.
4. **Feature Engagement**: $\ge 30\%$ member engagement with auxiliary features (AI Concierge, speaker portal).
5. **Post-Event Reconciliation**: Formal signed reconciliation statement finalized within 5 business days post-event.
