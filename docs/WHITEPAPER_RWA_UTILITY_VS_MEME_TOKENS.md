### 1. The Power of Soulbound Tokens (SBTs) for Member Identity

Traditional event ticketing relies on static PDF barcodes that are easily screenshotted, duplicated, and scalped on secondary markets at 300% markups, leaving event organizers with zero revenue and severe venue security liabilities.

Unykorn implements the **ERC-5192 Soulbound Token standard**:
1. **Non-Transferable Credentials:** Once issued to a verified member, the Digital Passport cannot be transferred or sold to third parties.
2. **Rotating Cryptographic QR Badges:** The pass generates a 15-minute salted cryptographic proof that venue staff validate in sub-seconds with zero personally identifiable information (PII) exposed.
3. **Admin Account Recovery:** If a VIP member loses access to their mobile device, authorized administrators can execute multi-sig cryptographic recovery without compromising ledger history.

### 2. Closed-Loop Prepaid Service Credits

Unykorn models member credits strictly as **closed-loop prepaid service credits** redeemable for approved educational programs, coaching hours, and summit seats:
* **Zero Speculation:** Credits possess no floating secondary market price and cannot be traded on public decentralized exchanges.
* **Deterministic Reconciliation:** Every credit issuance and redemption logs an immutable cryptographic receipt, guaranteeing zero accounting variance across daily financial audits.
* **Cash-Flow Acceleration:** Member pre-funding routes directly into dedicated venue escrow sub-accounts, eliminating six-figure personal credit float for event promoters.
