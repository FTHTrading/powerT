# ⚖️ Legal & Regulatory Partitioning — `powerT`

## 1. Regulatory De-risking Principles

Tokenizing personal likeness, name, or future open-ended consulting creates severe securities ambiguity. `powerT` circumvents this through **Three Core Tenets**:

1. **Service Prepayment vs. Investment Contract**:
   - The *Passport* and *Credits* represent prepaid access to measurable, scheduled business services (events, coaching, mastermind sessions, exhibitor booths).
   - Terms explicitly prohibit resale on secondary venues and disclaim any expectation of profit or appreciation.

2. **Isolated Bankruptcy-Remote SPV**:
   - The *PTI Revenue Note* is issued exclusively by a legally segregated SPV (e.g., `PTI Summit Series SPV LLC`).
   - The SPV holds specific assigned contractual receivables (e.g., Q4 National Summit sponsorship contracts).
   - Notes are offered solely to verified accredited investors under SEC Regulation D (Rule 506c) or international buyers under Regulation S.

3. **Howey Test Matrix Analysis**:

| Howey Prongs | Consumer Passport & Credit Layer | SPV Revenue Note Layer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Investment of Money** | Customer prepayment for goods & services. | Capital investment by accredited LPs. |
| **Common Enterprise** | No; individual consumer consumption. | Yes; pooled revenue rights held in SPV. |
| **Expectation of Profit** | Strictly zero; non-appreciating fixed utility. | Yes; defined yield / preferred return waterfall. |
| **Efforts of Others** | Customer redeems for personal benefit. | Yes; returns generated by event production & sales. |
| **Classification** | **Consumer Utility / Loyalty Asset** | **Regulated Tokenized Security** |
