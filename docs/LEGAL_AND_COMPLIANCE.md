# ⚖️ Legal & Regulatory Partitioning — `powerT`

## 1. Regulatory Status & Core Disclaimers

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **PROTOTYPE SPECIFICATION ONLY**: This repository contains architectural prototypes and product specifications for a controlled closed-loop utility pilot. It does not represent live financial infrastructure, an active securities offering, or a compliance determination.

### Required Terminology Standard
* **Prototype Restricted-Token Interface**: The experimental security token contracts represent prototype restricted-token interfaces, *not a compliance determination, issuance platform, or transfer-agent substitute*.
* **Potential Offering Pathways (Reg D / Reg S)**: References to Regulation D (Rule 506c) or Regulation S describe *potential offering pathways requiring issuer-specific securities counsel, offering documentation, investor eligibility controls, and jurisdictional review*.
* **Contractual Service Credit**: PTI Credits are *contractual closed-loop service credits with a published redemption schedule; not a stablecoin, deposit, stored-value account, or cash-equivalent*.
* **Settled Payment Reconciliation**: Credits are *issued only after verified settled payment and successful order reconciliation, with idempotency controls and manual exception review*.
* **Internal Settlement Prototype**: Settlement routing contracts represent an *internal settlement-control prototype; not third-party escrow, qualified custody, or fiduciary administration*.

## 2. Entity Structuring & Isolation

To ensure complete legal and financial separation between operational utility and any future institutional financing:

| Entity | Primary Function | Strictly Prohibited Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Powerteam Operating Co.** | Runs events, programs, membership, ticketing, customer support, and credit redemption. | Cannot offer investment returns through Passport/credit products. |
| **Powerteam IP Co.** | Holds licensed trademarks, curriculum, media rights, domains, and content licenses. | Cannot transfer rights it does not clearly own or control. |
| **PTI Event / SPV LLC** | Holds a precisely scheduled receivables or revenue-participation asset (if counsel approves). | Cannot commingle operating cash or promise generalized company profits. |
| **Independent Vendors** | Payment processor, KYC vendor, accountant, counsel, registered intermediaries. | Cannot be presented as unnecessary because a smart contract exists. |

## 4. Gates Required Prior to Future SPV Revenue Note Deployment
1. **Securities Counsel Engagement**: Issuer identification, exemption confirmation, offering memorandum (PPM), and resale restrictions.
2. **Bankruptcy-Remote Asset Assignment**: Formal assignment of scheduled, assignable subset of event receivables.
3. **Servicing Agreement Execution**: Executed operating agreement defining collections, permitted expenses, reserves, and default handling.
4. **Intermediary & Transfer Agent Review**: Broker-dealer/ATS, custody, and transfer-agent confirmation.
5. **Independent CPA Audit**: Historical collection validation and waterfall modeling.
6. **Accredited Investor Verification**: Third-party verification of accredited status under Rule 506(c).
